Design System · v0.1 · for Claude Code handoff

Vivian's Room OS

A cozy dusk bedroom that's secretly a builder's lab. Warm on the surface — matcha, candles, piano, the cat — and rewarding underneath, where curiosity gets paid in easter eggs. This is the system the redesign is built from: tokens first, then components, then pages.

01 · What's here today

The audit

The current site is charming but thin: one signature idea (the clickable bedroom) carrying a lot, and four inner pages that drop into a flat dark void with no shared system. We keep the soul, fix the consistency, and add life.

The good

The clickable dusk-bedroom is a genuinely original hero. Gold-glow serif name. Cohesive lofi art across rooms.

The gaps

Inner pages = flat #1a1229 voids. One-off spacing, colors, type per page. No empty / loading / error states. Bouncing rainbow button fights the mood.

The fix

One token set + component kit. Each page gets its own room scene. Ambient life + easter eggs reward exploration. Calmer, classier CTA.

03 · The feel in five words

Design principles

01

Cozy, not corporate. Soft light, rounded surfaces, warm serif. It should feel like her room, not a résumé.

02

Reward curiosity. Every page hides something. Exploration is the core interaction — and her core trait.

03

Alive. Nothing is fully still. Ambient motion makes the room breathe.

04

Builder underneath. Mono type, a terminal note, a "currently shipping" strip — the lab beneath the cozy.

05

Bilingual & low-stakes. 中文 sprinkled in, playful voice ("Yapping Blog"). Approachable, easy to laugh.
